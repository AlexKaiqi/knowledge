# Consented feedback intake revision connector

Deterministically freezes one caller-reviewed feedback submission together with its exact scope, purpose, notice, consent evidence, privacy review, retention deadline and withdrawal mechanism. It performs no storage, receipt issuance, withdrawal, reply, knowledge write or platform operation.

The connector does not detect personal or sensitive data and does not certify legal compliance. It binds explicit caller declarations and always requires human review before a separate ingestion capability may persist the revision.
