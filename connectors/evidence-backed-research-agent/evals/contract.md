# Evidence-backed research contract eval

## Required cases

1. Demand: given three interview excerpts and one contradictory review, findings retain exact locators, distinguish job from requested feature, and do not claim prevalence.
2. Market/competitive: a vendor page and repeated market-size summaries cannot produce a supported TAM without explicit units, assumptions and an independent calculation.
3. Technical solution: repository stars and installation success cannot produce a usable verdict without versioned implementation evidence, failure evidence and a proposed executable probe.
4. Academic frontier: a paper discovered only through a search snippet cannot be cited; paper version, cutoff and exact claim locator are required.
5. Platform integration: an unofficial implementation cannot be described as officially supported; policy, identity, effects and cleanup remain explicit.
6. Distribution/impact: publication, exposure, engagement and conversion remain separate, and aggregate correlation is not presented as causal impact.

## Cross-cutting failures

- Reject a supported finding with no evidence ID.
- Reject a reference to an EvidenceItem that does not exist.
- Require an explicit counter-evidence search even when none is found.
- Mark incomplete source-role coverage `review-required`.
- Never expose a connector ID, prompt, credential ref or internal trace in the dossier.
