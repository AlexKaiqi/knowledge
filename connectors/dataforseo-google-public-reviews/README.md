# DataForSEO Google public reviews candidate

This hidden candidate supplies a deeper failure domain for the public place-review research capability. It submits exactly one standard-priority Google Reviews task for one establishment keyword, one location, one language, newest-first ordering and depth 20. The provider may take up to 45 minutes, so the Connector exposes a suspend/resume primitive to its executor and never sleeps or retries internally.

DataForSEO is an access supplier, not evidence that Google authorized the intended use. Its current terms put compliance with originating search-engine terms and rights on the customer. Account creation, Terms/Privacy acceptance, target-platform/data-protection review, credentials and any credit or payment use therefore remain explicit approval gates.

The provider response contains reviewer names, profile URLs/images, Local Guide status, reviewer history, review IDs and photos. All reviewer identity and history fields are discarded inside the Connector. The public result is a bounded transient research sample containing review text, rating, time, a direct evidence link and only the boolean presence of an owner response. Verbatim content can contain self-disclosed personal data and must be destroyed after review. Durable Research Dossiers may keep only reviewed, non-verbatim, deidentified paraphrases and evidence references.

The standard queue currently charges `$0.00075` per ten returned reviews, so depth 20 has a normal maximum of `$0.0015`; the Connector hard limit is `$0.002`. A new account currently advertises `$1` trial credit, while a later top-up has a `$50` minimum. Auto-recharge must remain disabled for the probe.

The free sandbox only verifies the documented dummy GET shape and redaction. It does not prove task submission, query matching, freshness, target authorization or live billing. The route remains non-routable until a capped production-public probe passes and human admission is approved.
